Project Overview

Role: Graphic Designer Intern
Company: United Hotel Supply
Project Type: Architectural Signage & Technical Catalog Sheets
Responsibilities: Layout architecture, material callout diagramming, part number indexing, symbol vector styling, and accessibility compliance formatting.

The Brief

Develop a comprehensive catalog series for hotel architectural signage. The sheets needed to clearly organize guest room numbers, directional wayfinding markers, ADA-compliant accessibility symbols, and regulatory in-room safety plaques for commercial hotel developments.

The Design Challenge

Hospitality signage involves dense technical parameters—such as acrylic material layer callouts, exact millimeter and inch dimensions, grade II tactile braille, and standardized ADA symbols (ISA & Hearing Impaired). The main challenge was maintaining a clean grid structure across varying sign dimensions while displaying SKU part numbers unambiguously.

My Role & Responsibilities

• Engineered consistent sheet templates featuring standardized red header banners and light blue backgrounds.
• Created technical schematic callouts diagramming material layers (P95 Matte acrylic, raised copy, Grade II Bead Braille, and Wilsonart laminate).
• Organized product grids with clear part numbers, dimension callouts, and icon variations.
• Designed vector icon badges for accessibility options (ISA wheel chair symbol, hearing impairment, and non-smoking icons).
• Formatted emergency evacuation plan templates and warning plaque diagrams to legal and safety standards.

01 — Tactile Room Numbering System (P95 Acrylic Series)

Features the core room numbering scheme with detailed engineering callouts (Part# CWS19-1000). Includes technical callouts for 1/8" P95 Matte Clear Acrylic, 1-1/2" raised lettering in PMS 9186, Clear Grade II Bead Braille, and Wilsonart Palisades Oak Laminate accents. Sub-variants highlight ISA and Hearing Impaired symbol integration.

02 — In-Room Regulatory & Safety Signage

Covers essential guest room compliance signs, including 8.5"×11" printed evacuation maps (Part# SC-1226), curved deadbolt directional indicators, sprinkler prohibition safety stickers, and eco-friendly towel reuse station cards with customizable branding.

03 — Wayfinding & Directional Navigation Systems

Presents a complete line of directional signs ranging from 2-line small hallway markers to 10-line comprehensive lobby directional boards (REI20 Series). Features standardized typography, clear directional arrow iconography, and wall-mounted flag sign layouts.

04 — Modern Rounded Room Number Series

Demonstrates a contemporary curved-corner aesthetic (HDI124 Series) available in dark slate grey and light sand tones. Showcases multiple icon configurations including No Smoking, Accessibility (ISA), and Hearing Impaired combination badges.

05 — Emergency Evacuation & Guest Warning Plaques

Highlights large-format emergency evacuation notices (23"×10" Part# TWP2019-1226) displaying California Civil Codes alongside fire and safety instructions. Also features compact balcony lock warnings, in-room safe instructions, and sprinkler safety decals.

What I Learned

• Precise technical callout diagrams are critical for hotel developers and installation contractors.
• Strict alignment systems allow dense catalog sheets with dozens of SKUs to remain easily scannable.
• Standardizing symbol placement (ADA, ISA, Hearing Impaired) ensures design consistency across full signage lines.

Professional Takeaway

Designing architectural signage collateral for United Hotel Supply deepened my mastery of grid-based graphic systems, technical drawing callouts, and ADA accessibility compliance in commercial print production.
