Contexa

An AI-powered meeting workspace that transforms meetings into AI summaries, searchable transcripts, action items, and team knowledge.

View Case Study ↓ Live Prototype

Overview

Contexa is an AI-powered meeting workspace that integrates with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams to automatically transform meetings into organized knowledge. Instead of manually reviewing recordings, teams receive AI-generated summaries, action items, searchable transcripts, and contextual answers in one collaborative workspace.

Problem

Teams spend countless hours revisiting meeting recordings, searching through scattered notes, and following up on missed action items. Important decisions often become buried across documents, chats, and emails, making collaboration slow and knowledge difficult to retrieve.

Goal

Design a clean enterprise SaaS experience that enables users to understand meeting outcomes within seconds, collaborate efficiently, and retrieve organizational knowledge instantly using AI.

Design Approach

The interface was intentionally designed around clarity rather than feature density. Every screen prioritizes readability, generous whitespace, consistent spacing, and familiar document-style layouts inspired by products like Notion, Linear, and Vercel.

The experience focuses on:

• AI-first workflows
• Large whitespace
• Fast information retrieval
• Clear visual hierarchy
• Consistent design system
• Enterprise-grade usability

Landing Page

The landing page introduces Contexa through a clean, product-first marketing experience. The design emphasizes the value proposition with clear messaging, generous whitespace, modern typography, and focused call-to-actions inspired by premium enterprise SaaS products.

Onboarding Experience

The onboarding flow minimizes friction by allowing users to quickly sign in and connect their preferred meeting platforms including Google Meet, Zoom, and Microsoft Teams before entering the workspace.

Meeting Workspace

The Meeting Workspace is the core experience of Contexa. It brings together AI-generated summaries, key decisions, action items, participants, transcripts, and contextual AI assistance within a single distraction-free interface. The layout was designed to feel familiar to users of modern document-based tools while keeping important information easy to scan.

AI Chat Assistant

The AI Chat interface enables users to ask natural language questions about previous meetings and instantly receive contextual answers, summaries, decisions, and follow-up suggestions without searching through long transcripts manually.

Universal Search

The universal search experience allows users to instantly find meetings, tasks, people, projects, and transcripts from a single interface. Results are grouped logically, making information retrieval fast and reducing the time spent navigating between multiple pages.

Dashboard

The dashboard provides a focused overview of today's meetings, recent AI summaries, and pending action items. Rather than overwhelming users with charts or analytics, it presents only the information needed to start the workday efficiently.

Design System

The interface follows a minimal enterprise design language with consistent spacing, rounded components, accessible typography, subtle shadows, and reusable UI patterns that ensure visual consistency across every screen.

Key UX Decisions

• Prioritized readability over visual complexity.

• Designed AI as a contextual assistant instead of a separate experience.

• Reduced navigation depth to improve discoverability.

• Used generous whitespace to lower cognitive load.

• Maintained a consistent spacing system and card layout across every screen.

• Focused on document-style layouts familiar to modern knowledge workers.

Outcome

Contexa demonstrates how AI can become a natural extension of meeting workflows rather than simply generating summaries. The concept combines meeting documentation, collaboration, search, and AI assistance into a single scalable workspace suitable for modern enterprise teams.

What I Learned

Designing Contexa strengthened my understanding of enterprise SaaS products, AI-assisted user experiences, information architecture, design systems, and creating interfaces that balance functionality with simplicity.
