Project Overview

Role: Graphic Designer Intern
Company: United Hotel Supply
Project Type: Commercial Product Catalogue / Hospitality Marketing Collateral
Responsibilities: Page layout, product presentation, typography hierarchy, spec formatting, spacing alignment, revision corrections, and PDF production exports.

The Brief

The assignment was to produce commercial product catalogue sheets for hotel lighting programs (including hotel brands like Courtyard by Marriott, Fairfield Inn & Suites, Super 8 by Wyndham, Spark by Hilton, and Tru by Hilton). The product specs, images, and text content were provided via reference materials. My goal was to synthesize these provided assets into structured, presentation-ready catalogue layouts tailored for commercial client review.

The Design Challenge

The core challenge was taking large volumes of highly repetitive product information—product codes, physical dimensions, material/finish tags, and item photos—and arranging them into a clear, usable layout system. Rather than inventing arbitrary aesthetics, the job demanded strict visual consistency across varied fixture quantities so that clients could effortlessly scan specifications.

My Role & Responsibilities

• Organized provided raw text, codes, and dimensions into systematic product cards.
• Set up clear typographic hierarchies separating brand headers, product codes, dimensions, and finish tags.
• Cropped and placed product fixture imagery consistently inside designated card boundaries.
• Maintained equal spacing, alignment, and grid structure across multi-item pages.
• Applied revisions based on content corrections and exported high-resolution PDFs for production and distribution.

Design Process

Reference Materials → Content & Asset Structuring → Grid & Card Layout → Typography & Tag Styling → Revisions & Alignment Fixes → Final PDF Production Export

Visual Design Decisions

• Information Hierarchy: Established strong contrast using bold headers, dark rounded product cards, and highlighted callout badges.
• Micro-Typography & Tags: Applied pill-style finish badges (e.g., "Brushed Nickel", "Matte Black", "Wood") to separate technical specifications from product names visually.
• Structured Grid System: Employed a consistent three-column card grid to keep varied fixture types cohesive.
• Brand Identity Placement: Integrated client brand logos alongside United Hotel Supply contact footers and quick-quote callouts.

01 — Header Hierarchy & Brand-Specific Layout (Courtyard by Marriott)

This layout establishes the header hierarchy for the Courtyard by Marriott Lighting Program. My work focused on balancing brand identity elements—such as the hotel logo and lighting program tag—with a 3-column product grid featuring curated fixtures, clear dimensions, material badges, and prominent company contact details.

02 — Color Palette Adjustments & Finish Badge Styling (Fairfield Inn & Suites)

For the Fairfield Inn & Suites sheet, the layout required incorporating specific material tags such as "Painted Antique Brass" across diverse fixture shapes (pendant, flush mount, wall sconces, and mirrors). I managed the spacing to accommodate 15 curated fixtures without cluttering the page structure.

03 — Multi-Row Consistency & Custom Finish Highlights (Super 8 Guestroom)

This spread for the Super 8 by Wyndham lighting program contains a dense 15-fixture layout across five rows. My responsibility was maintaining exact vertical and horizontal alignment, ensuring that variations like custom color tags ("Match Sherwin Williams", "Blue Poly") remained visually legible across repeated card components.

04 — Layout Adaptability for Varied Content Density (Spark Spring Scheme)

The Spark Spring Scheme by Hilton required fitting 14 fixtures along with an inline company logo callout block. I adjusted the card flow around the contact block while maintaining readable typography for complex product specifications and dimensions.

05 — High-Density Layout & Final Production Polish (Tru by Hilton)

Featuring 16 curated fixtures, this Tru by Hilton page demonstrates high-density layout management. I ensured that despite the large number of items, clear padding around images and distinct pill tags ("Satin Nickel", "Red", "Teal") prevented visual overload, producing a clean final production output.

What I Learned

• Commercial layout design requires rigorous discipline in consistency, alignment, and spacing.
• Managing repetitive specifications demands clear typographic hierarchy and functional UI components like tags/badges.
• Designing within client specifications and existing assets is a fundamental production skill in real-world graphic design.
• Revisions and visual precision are vital when preparing multi-page commercial collateral.

Professional Takeaway

This internship project at United Hotel Supply reinforced my technical layout skills and appreciation for production accuracy. Working within rigid brand guidelines and structured product data taught me how thoughtful graphic design transforms complex catalog information into polished, commercial-ready presentation materials.
